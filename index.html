Concert guide · December 20, 2026

Handel's Messiah

A guided journey through prophecy, suffering, and triumph. Follow the exact chapter times in the Royal Melbourne Philharmonic's free complete performance.

Before you listen

Handel composed Messiah in 1741; it premiered in Dublin in 1742. An oratorio is an extended work for voices and orchestra, generally presented without costumes or acting. Listen for recitative (speech-like singing), aria (expressive solo), chorus (ensemble), and word painting (music reflecting a word's meaning).

Compare an interpretation
